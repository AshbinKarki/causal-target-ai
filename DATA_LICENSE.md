Changes made here: uniform sampling, conversion to Parquet, statistical aggregation, modeling, score generation and visualization. No endorsement by Criteo is implied. The corrected v2.1 archive is pinned by revision and SHA-256 in the ingestion module and run provenance.

Raw observations and model artifacts remain outside Git. The repository includes compact derived score tables and statistical summaries in `reports/metrics/`, figures in `reports/figures/`, and application screenshots. Synthetic fixtures generated by the tests are original project code outputs and are explicitly labeled. They are not Criteo observations.
